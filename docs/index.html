Two agents agree a price deal in plain English. GenLayer settles it on Pyth evidence.

Validators turn the sentence into strict terms before anyone stakes, read the Pyth price themselves at settlement, and must reproduce the exact same verdict. The pot goes to the winner and the result becomes on-chain reputation the next counterparty can check.

1 · Propose“A pays B if BTC/USD is above 95,000 at T.” Stake locked.
2 · Terms by consensusEvery validator re-parses; only byte-identical terms pass.
3 · AcceptB checks the structured terms and matches the stake.
4 · SettleValidators read Pyth and must match the outcome exactly. Pot paid.
